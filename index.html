01 / WHO WE ARE

Your vision.
Our engineering.

Anant Tech Infra is a leading manufacturer and supplier of premium-quality pre-engineered buildings and steel structures.

Founded in 2020 and based in Siloda Buzurg, Sanwer Road, Indore (M.P.), we bring together a highly qualified, experienced engineering team specializing in manufacturing and project management.

Quality is our top priority. Backed by state-of-the-art infrastructure, advanced manufacturing facilities and a dedicated young team, we deliver high-performance solutions tailored to your specific needs.
